# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email hei@tazk.no. Do not open a public issue.

## Remediation time frames

| Severity | Fixed within |
|---|---|
| Critical, high | 7 days |
| Medium | 30 days |
| Low | Next release |

Dependencies follow the same table from the day an advisory (`npm audit`, Dependabot alert) names them. Other updates: at least monthly.
